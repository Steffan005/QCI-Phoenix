## ARTICLE II: SDVOSB STATUS AND VERIFICATION

### Section 2.1 - Service-Disabled Veteran Ownership
The Company qualifies as a **Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)** under:
- 38 U.S.C. § 8127
- 13 CFR Part 125
- FAR Subpart 19.14

### Section 2.2 - Ownership Requirements
To maintain SDVOSB certification:

**(a) Ownership**
- One or more service-disabled veterans must unconditionally own at least **51%** of the Company
- The sole Member, **Steffan Douglas Haskins**, owns **100%** of the Company
- This satisfies all SDVOSB ownership requirements

**(b) Control**
- Service-disabled veterans must control the management and daily operations
- **Steffan Douglas Haskins** serves as sole Managing Member
- All major decisions require approval of service-disabled veteran owner

**(c) Service-Disabled Veteran Status**
```
Member Name:       Steffan Douglas Haskins
Veteran Status:    United States Armed Forces Veteran
Disability Rating: 100% Permanent and Total (P&T)
VA Documentation:  [On file / To be attached]
```

### Section 2.3 - Certification
The Company shall seek and maintain verification/certification from:
- U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) VetBiz Registry
- System for Award Management (SAM.gov)
- California Department of General Services (DGS) DVBE
- Applicable state and local SDVOSB/DVBE programs

### Section 2.4 - Prohibition on Ownership Transfer
No ownership interest in the Company may be transferred, sold, or encumbered in any manner that would:
- Reduce service-disabled veteran ownership below 51%
- Transfer control to a non-service-disabled veteran
- Compromise SDVOSB certification status

The Company shall maintain active registrations with:

The Company shall obtain and maintain a UEI for federal contracting.

### Section 7.3 - Set-Aside Eligibility
As an SDVOSB, the Company is eligible for:
- SDVOSB sole-source contracts (up to $5M for services)
- SDVOSB set-aside competitive contracts
- VA Rule of Two preference
- DoD small business set-asides
- Subcontracting opportunities with large primes

### Section 7.4 - Target Agencies
Primary target agencies for government contracting:

| Agency | Opportunity |
|--------|-------------|
| Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) | Healthcare AI, claims processing |
| Department of Defense (DoD) | Autonomous systems, C4ISR |
| Defense Innovation Unit (DIU) | Emerging technology |
| Intelligence Community | Classified applications |
| NASA | Space systems, autonomous navigation |
| DHS | Border security, threat detection |

### Section 7.5 - Sole-Source Authority
Under 38 U.S.C. § 8127(c), the VA may award **sole-source contracts** to SDVOSBs for:
- Contracts up to $5,000,000 for services
