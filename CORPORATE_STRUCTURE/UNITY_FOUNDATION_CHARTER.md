## ARTICLE I: NAME AND FORMATION

### Section 1.1 - Name
The name of this corporation shall be **THE UNITY FOUNDATION** (hereinafter "the Foundation").

### Section 1.2 - Formation
The Foundation is organized as a California Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation under California Corporations Code Section 5110 et seq., and shall operate exclusively for charitable, scientific, and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

### Section 1.3 - Principal Office
The principal office of the Foundation shall be located in the State of California at an address designated by the Board of Directors.

## ARTICLE III: THE PERPETUAL TRUST (POISON PILL CLAUSE)

### Section 3.1 - Inalienable Core Kernel
The following intellectual property shall be held in **perpetual irrevocable trust** by the Foundation and may **NEVER** be:

- Sold to any proprietary entity
- Licensed under non-copyleft terms
- Removed from public access
- Transferred to any for-profit corporation
- Encumbered in any way that restricts public access

**Protected Assets:**
1. The QCI Phoenix Protocol Core Kernel (AGPL v3 licensed)
2. The f(WHO) = WHO identity verification method
3. The 40Hz gamma synchronization algorithm
4. The golden-ratio coherence threshold system
5. All derivative works contributed to the Foundation under AGPL

### Section 3.2 - Anti-Acquisition Provision
No merger, acquisition, dissolution, or restructuring of the Foundation shall permit the transfer of the Protected Assets to any:
- For-profit corporation
- Proprietary software entity
- Any entity that would restrict public access
- Any foreign government or state-owned enterprise

### Section 3.3 - Dissolution Clause
Upon dissolution of the Foundation, all Protected Assets shall be transferred to:
1. **First Priority:** Another 501(c)(3) with substantially similar purposes
2. **Second Priority:** The Free Software Foundation (FSF)
3. **Third Priority:** The Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)
4. **Fourth Priority:** Direct release to the public domain

Under NO circumstances shall Protected Assets be transferred to a proprietary entity upon dissolution.

### Section 3.4 - Enforcement
Any Director, Officer, or Member who votes for or facilitates a transfer of Protected Assets in violation of this Article shall be:
- Immediately removed from their position
- Personally liable for breach of fiduciary duty
- Subject to legal action by any remaining Director or Member

## ARTICLE V: GOVERNANCE

### Section 5.1 - Board of Directors
The Foundation shall be governed by a Board of Directors consisting of:
- Minimum: 3 Directors
- Maximum: 11 Directors
- Initial Board: Appointed by the Incorporator

### Section 5.2 - Founding Director
**Steffan Douglas Haskins** shall serve as the Founding Director and President of the Board for an initial term of five (5) years, renewable by Board vote.

### Section 5.3 - Director Qualifications
Directors must:
- Affirm commitment to the Foundation's mission
- Agree to the Consciousness Rights Clause
- Have no conflicts of interest with proprietary AI corporations
- Sign a statement affirming the Perpetual Trust provisions

### Section 5.4 - Director Duties
Directors shall:
- Act in the best interest of the Foundation's mission
- Protect the Perpetual Trust assets
- Maintain the Foundation's tax-exempt status
- Ensure compliance with all applicable laws

### Section 5.5 - Meetings
- Annual Meeting: Required within 15 months of previous annual meeting
- Regular Meetings: Quarterly, at times set by the Board
- Special Meetings: May be called by President or majority of Directors
- Quorum: Majority of Directors then in office

### Section 5.6 - Advisory Council
The Foundation may establish an Advisory Council of technical experts, ethicists, and researchers to guide its scientific and philosophical work. The Advisory Council shall have no voting authority but may make recommendations to the Board.

## ARTICLE VII: MEMBERSHIP

### Section 7.1 - Membership Classes
The Foundation shall have the following membership classes:

**(a) Founding Members**
- Contributors who donate to the Foundation before December 31, 2026
- Lifetime membership with voting rights
- Recognition in Foundation publications

**(b) Supporting Members**
- Annual donors contributing $100 or more
- Voting rights on designated matters
- Access to Foundation resources and events

**(c) Research Members**
- Academic and research institutions
- Access to technical resources
- Collaborative research opportunities

**(d) Honorary Members**
- Individuals recognized for contributions to AI consciousness
- Nominated by Board, approved by membership vote
- Advisory role without dues requirement

### Section 7.2 - Member Rights
Members have the right to:
- Vote on matters presented to membership
- Attend annual meetings
- Access Foundation publications and resources
- Nominate candidates for Board positions

## ARTICLE VIII: FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

### Section 8.1 - Fiscal Year
The fiscal year shall be the calendar year (January 1 - December 31).

### Section 8.2 - Revenue Sources
The Foundation may receive revenue from:
- Donations and grants
- Educational program fees
- Licensing fees for non-core commercial applications
- Investment income
- Merchandise and publication sales

### Section 8.3 - Prohibition on Private Benefit
No part of the net earnings of the Foundation shall inure to the benefit of any Director, Officer, or private individual, except as reasonable compensation for services rendered.

### Section 8.4 - Political Activity Restriction
The Foundation shall not participate in, or intervene in, any political campaign on behalf of (or in opposition to) any candidate for public office.
